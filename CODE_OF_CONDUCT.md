# Code of Conduct

This project is maintained by one person in their spare time. To keep it a good place
to contribute:

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to open source.
- Critique code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Don't spam the repo with low-effort PRs or comments asking to be assigned or merged.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and PRs, and block users who
don't follow these rules. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer
through their GitHub profile ([@imlakshay08](https://github.com/imlakshay08)).
